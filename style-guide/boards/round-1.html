3Typeface

Which font family (or pairing) does eox-vote use?

Poppins: site (layout.tsx:5-9, 400-900, and .impeccable.md:16 "Poppins only"), MonMap (layout.tsx:12-18), Jobs (layout.tsx:47-50). Inter/system: MAC Study (globals.css:93-100, never loaded, so Windows shows Segoe UI). Checked in the font files: Poppins has no tnum feature and its digits have different widths (320-635 units), so tabular-nums does nothing and live counts jitter. Inter has tnum. In the tally, every number has the same digit count: with tabular figures their left edges line up, with Poppins they stagger.

A - Poppins onlysite, MonMap, Jobs
